# typoscope

A digital reading window for PDFs: drag and resize a rectangle over the page to 
isolate one line or block of text at a time, with everything outside it dimmed. 

Visual snow is a rare post-concussion phenomena in which someone sees constant
static, or "snow" around pattens and light in their visual field. This tool is 
an aid for those experiencing visual snow or other neurological disabilities 
that make hinders their reading ability.
