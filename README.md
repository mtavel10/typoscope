# typoscope

Visual snow is a rare post-concussion phenomena in which someone sees constant
static or fuzz around pattens and light in their visual field. This tool is 
an aid for those experiencing visual snow or other neurological disabilities 
that make hinders their reading ability. 

A typoscope is a panel placed over text to isolate one line at a time, allowing
one to focus while reading and limit the visual fuzz. 

To use this digital typoscope, simply upload your pdf. Then, drag and resize 
the reading window so that one line is view, and the rest of the text is dimmed. 
Scroll to read one line at a time, snow free!
